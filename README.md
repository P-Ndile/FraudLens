## 📌 Project Overview

**FraudLens** is a cybersecurity-focused hackathon prototype designed to help South African small, medium and micro enterprises (SMMEs) identify potential invoice fraud, suspicious supplier banking changes, and social engineering indicators.

Developed during the **HackSecure Innovation Challenge 2026**, FraudLens explores how accessible digital tools can help small businesses make safer payment decisions without requiring advanced cybersecurity knowledge.

The solution focuses on affordability, usability, and practical fraud prevention for businesses with limited security resources.

> **Project status:** Completed hackathon demonstration prototype. FraudLens is not a production-ready financial security service.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Many small businesses face cybersecurity challenges that can result in financial losses, operational disruption, and reduced customer trust.

Invoice fraud is particularly concerning because criminals may impersonate legitimate suppliers or manipulate payment instructions.

Common risks include:

- Fraudulent changes to supplier banking details
- Invoice manipulation and supplier impersonation
- Urgent payment requests designed to pressure employees
- Limited cybersecurity awareness and verification processes
- Lack of affordable fraud detection tools for smaller businesses

**Our challenge:** How can we make invoice fraud detection more accessible to South African SMMEs?

---

## 💡 Our Solution

FraudLens proposes a simple workflow that helps businesses assess suspicious invoices before authorising payments.

### 🔍 1. Invoice Scanning
Upload a PDF invoice for analysis of relevant payment information and potential fraud indicators.

### 🏦 2. Supplier Verification
Maintain reference supplier information and compare invoice banking details against the expected account information.

### 🚩 3. Risk Indicators
Identify suspicious banking changes and potentially manipulative language, such as urgent payment instructions.

### 📊 4. Risk Assessment Dashboard
Present risk indicators and recommended verification actions in an accessible interface.

### 📁 5. Case Management
Provide a workflow for reviewing suspicious invoices and recording verification decisions.

**Prototype note:** These capabilities describe the proposed FraudLens solution. The hackathon demonstration and repository may contain simulated workflows, partially implemented features, or development-stage integrations.

## 🔐 Security Approach

FraudLens was designed around several important cybersecurity principles:

- **Supplier baseline verification:** Compare submitted invoice details with previously recorded supplier information.
- **Risk-based assessment:** Flag suspicious changes rather than automatically approving payments.
- **Human verification:** Encourage users to independently confirm banking changes with suppliers.
- **Access control:** Explore authenticated, business-specific access to supplier and invoice records.
- **Secure development:** Consider input validation, authentication, API protection, and data access restrictions.

### ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

FraudLens is a demonstration prototype created for a cybersecurity hackathon.

It must not be relied upon to approve financial transactions or verify legitimate banking details. Automated indicators can produce false positives or miss sophisticated fraud attempts.

Businesses should independently verify suspicious payment instructions through trusted communication channels.
